03 / BUILT TO LEAVE YOUR HANDS

A file you keep.
A guide they can use.

Relay runs in your browser. Your guides and photos are stored on this device; they aren’t uploaded to an account. Export an editable backup, or a ready-to-follow offline guide.

Every exported guide carries a version fingerprint. The walkthrough record remembers which path someone took and any notes they chose to leave.

What Relay checks — and what it doesn’t

Before you run or export a guide, Relay checks for missing destinations, unreachable steps, loops, and images without descriptions. It cannot check whether your instructions are correct. Test your guide with the person who will use it.

Local storage can be cleared by your browser. Keep a JSON backup. Offline guides include the text and photos you put in them; share them intentionally. Walkthrough records are self-reported, not proof of work.
